## What it is

Institutional-finance trust rendered as restraint: a pale, cool day canvas for reading and comparing, grounded by deep-ink night tones where the brand needs gravity.
Duality is structural, not a dark-mode toggle — full-bleed band flips, a night-toned nav and footer frame, or a fully light banking surface; never a mid-gray blend between worlds.
One deep trust accent (cobalt through violet) carries the single conversion action per view, while money renders in tabular or mono figures aligned like ledger columns.
Depth is tonal, display presence comes from scale rather than stacked weight, and visible fine print signals candor — poise over persuasion.

## Distinguishing moves

- **Duality check:** two worlds, day and night — white sections plus near-black night bands (hero, closing CTA) or a night-toned chrome frame on light pages; an all-day variant is legal, but the two worlds never blend into mid-tones.
- **Scarcity check:** exactly one filled accent pill per view — the open-account or get-started action; every secondary action is a translucent or soft-gray capsule, never a second saturated fill.
- **Numeric check:** every price, balance, ticker, and delta renders in mono or tabular figures with column alignment; up/down readability comes from color on text — green/red carries deltas and success, never buttons or section fills.
- **Weight check:** display presence comes from size (up to ~5rem) and tight tracking, not stacking — display runs mid-weight (400–600) by default; a bold (700) display face is legal only if every non-display role stays ≤600.
- **Geometry check:** one button geometry per build — pills, or a single 10–12px radius — applied to every action; never mixed radii on interactive elements, never sharp corners on CTAs.
- **Elevation check:** depth is tonal — white cards on gray bands, lifted near-black on night canvas, 1px dividers — and any shadow stays whisper-level (≤4% opacity, no stacks).
- **Tier check:** the recommended plan is marked by inversion only (a night fill or the single accent fill); no badges, ribbons, or accent borders.
- **Candor check:** regulatory and legal fine print sits at the page base in small muted type — visible by default, never collapsed away.

## Accent and color budget

≤2 chromatic events per viewport: the one accent action plus link and delta colors.
Trust accents are deep, saturated blue-to-violet hues — cobalt, indigo, royal, violet; declare one and never two.
Green and red stay semantic — text first, whisper-tint badges at most; never a saturated fill, button, or chart chrome.
Big stat numerals stay unaccented — scale and ink carry them, not color.
The accent never washes a large surface except the lone featured-tier inversion; night bands stay ink-dark, not accent-tinted.

## Keep / Change / Do-not-copy

| Split | Pre-written guidance |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | The day/night world structure; one deep accent action with translucent secondaries; tabular money type; tonal elevation with whisper shadows; featured-tier inversion; visible fine print. |
| **Change** | Accent depth within the blue-to-violet family; button geometry dialect (pill versus 10–12px radius); day-surface temperature; night-canvas cast (neutral near-black to ink-violet); the mono face. |
| **Do-not-copy** | Logos, literal screenshots, exact trade dress, any single source's full token set — including its licensed display faces, its luminous 3D illustration system, or its wide market-card palette. |

## Where it fits

- **saas-landing** — financial products: exchanges, banking, treasury tools, B2B payments; pricing pages are the genre's showpiece.
- **dashboard** — light-mode banking and trading surfaces: ledgers, balance tables, tabular figures.
- **docs-page** — developer and API platforms: the day world with mono numerics.
- **onboarding** — account-opening flows: rounded actions, numeric keypads, trust copy, one action per screen.
- **mobile app** — banking apps where calm reads as security.
- **desktop app** — treasury and ops consoles where density rises but poise holds.
- Regulated-product marketing lands here too: disclosures, risk copy, and rate tables set in the same quiet grid as the pitch.
- Night-only variant is legal — a full night canvas with lifted night cards — keep the same budget either way.
- Moods served: professional_minimal, trust, precision, premium restraint — the corporate card lands here.
- Not for playful consumer fintech (playful-pop), dense trading terminals (neon-terminal), or warm narrative brands (warm-editorial).

## Blends

- With quiet-saas for light product interiors — inherit its gray ladder; finance adds the darker ink, rounded actions, and tabular numerics.
- Atop bento-grid for pricing and comparison card layouts.
- With noir-technical when the brief wants the night world to take over the whole product.
